About this build

Compare v0.1: plain JavaScript with no libraries. Everything runs on this device and images never leave it.

Known limits: matching takes a few seconds and the page pauses meanwhile (Any angle is much slower). It has only been tested on the synthetic samples here. The proportion score is sometimes missing, and photos of a tilted page aren't flattened yet.
