tools for artists
squint

2026 · design and code

see your reference the way painters squint at it. a free tool that blurs and posterizes photos into simple value shapes, so you can nail proportions before the details. it started on art subreddits, where i was breaking people's references into value shapes by hand. works on your phone or computer, and nothing gets uploaded.

open squint

flicker

2026 · design and code

a flip test for artists. load your drawing and your reference, then pan, zoom, and rotate the reference to line it up. flip between them to spot what's off, and export a gif.

open flicker
